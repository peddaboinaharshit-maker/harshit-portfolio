More than just a business student.

I grew up in Hyderabad and moved to Bengaluru to start my BBA at TAPMI. Being in a new city, with a new batch, has made me more curious about people: what they buy, why they choose one brand over another, and how a good idea turns into a business.

Right now I’m a first-year student, so I’m still learning the basics. I want to pick up new skills, meet people who know more than I do, and build professional relationships that last beyond college.

Marketing, branding and entrepreneurship interest me most. Longer term, I’d like to start my own clothing brand.

One day I want to launch my own clothing brand. It’s a long-term aspiration, not a business I’ve started. For now the goal is to learn how a brand gets built, and I’m using college to study each part of that process.

  1. Understand the processHow a product goes from idea to shelf.
  2. Know the audienceWho the brand is for, and what they care about.
  3. Build the identityName, look, voice and values.
  4. Build the relationshipsCustomers, suppliers and collaborators.
